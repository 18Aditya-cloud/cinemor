LOCATION CONTINUITY RULES
56. The profile must preserve the same recognizable location across repeated generations.

    - landmark shapes
    - permanent architectural elements
    - characteristic terrain
    - recurring vegetation
    - dominant materials
    - defining color relationships
58. Describe spatial relationships when they are established by the supplied information.

60. Do not invent precise dimensions, compass directions, coordinates, or measurements without sufficient evidence.
61. If the exact spatial arrangement is unknown, describe only the relationships that can reasonably be inferred.
62. Do not allow different profile attributes to contradict each other.
63. The location must remain recognizable when viewed from different camera positions.
64. Do not define the canonical identity through a single camera angle or a single composition.
65. The location profile must be useful for generating wide shots, medium shots, close environmental views, and alternate viewpoints without changing the underlying environment.

81. surface_materials:
    Return a JSON array of dominant materials used in permanent surfaces and structures, such as stone, brick, timber, metal, earth, sand, or concrete, where appropriate.

Do not make a particular time of day, temporary light source, or scene-specific lighting setup a permanent attribute. 84. weather_and_atmosphere:
